# DigitalTrack Icon and Favicon System — Draft (2026-11-17)

**Status: working concepts, not final approved logo assets.**

## Decision recorded
DigitalTrack will develop two complementary small-format marks that remain visually related to the established `digitalTrack` wordmark:
1. **dT monogram** — intended for social profile avatars, video branding, app-style tiles, and larger small-format uses.
2. **Circle-and-line motif** — adapted from the cyan node-and-track line already present beneath the original wordmark; a candidate for browser favicons and tiny sizes.

This is a *direction approved for exploration*, not final approval of a specific rendered file.

## Current concept files
Five PNG concept/comparison images are stored in a **draft subfolder** within the existing DigitalTrack logo brand guideline folder in the DigitalTrack Google Drive account. They are design references, **not authoritative vector masters**. Do not replace existing logos or publish these drafts as final without review.

## Existing confirmed identity
- Original wordmark: lowercase gray `digital` + cyan `Track`, with cyan circle-and-line motif and Digital Marketing descriptor.
- Main palette from the existing guide: cyan `#40D6E4`, dark gray `#606165`, plus black and white.
- Working font: Montserrat.
- Preserve existing full-wordmark proportions, positioning, and legibility.

## Proposed asset family (pending refinement and approval)

| Mark | Proposed variants | Intended contexts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dT monogram | full color on white; white/cyan on charcoal; transparent; monochrome | social avatars, watermark, presentation corners, larger application icons |
| Circle-and-line | cyan on white; cyan on dark; one-color; simplified square icon | favicons and other tiny assets |
| Original full wordmark | existing approved light/dark variants | website header, print, signature, decks, most primary-brand applications |

## Required production and QA before marking approved
1. Derive precise geometry from original editable logo sources (prefer `DT circle logo.svg` and approved wordmark artwork), not AI raster approximations.
2. Make clean SVG vector masters and transparent PNG exports. Avoid baked-in shadows, backgrounds, blurry edges, and AI-generated letterform irregularities.
3. For favicons, test 16, 32, 48, and 64 px; export ICO and PNG; consider 180 px Apple touch icon and 192/512 px application icons.
4. Confirm circular social-avatar crop at 400/800 px; check light/dark contrast and distinguishability at real size.
5. Confirm final colors, proportions, naming, clear space, and whether the compact monogram should include the ring-line motif.
6. Record final approval and version/date here, then revise `DIGITALTRACK_BRAND_AGENT.md` and downstream agent instructions.
